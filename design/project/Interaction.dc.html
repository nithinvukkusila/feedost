How things respond.

A button is a stamp block: pressing pushes it into its own shadow, in 80 milliseconds, with no fade. Focus is a thick blue ring set off from the edge so it shows on yellow, red and black alike.

Rest
Shadow 5px right and down.
Pressed
Moves into the shadow. A short buzz on phones that support it.
Keyboard focus
3px blue ring, 4px away from the edge. On blue or black grounds the ring is yellow.
Working
Stays pressed and says what is happening. No spinner.
Not available
Dashed outline, no shadow, and a line beneath saying why.
