# R1-10: "I've paid" claims and the notifications inbox

Slice R1-10 (docs/BUILD_PLAN.md section 5, size M by the plan, depends on R1-9 and R1-7). Written 2026-10-15. Money-adjacent and the first time a parent writes anything to us, so the full process applies. Nothing here moves money: a claim is a parent telling the tutor "I paid", and only the tutor's tap on Confirm records a payment.

Tags: [unit] Vitest, [e2e] Playwright at 360px with stubbed hosts, [db] pgTAP, [fn] Edge Function test (Node with fakes, as `receipt` and `pay-page`), [manual] by hand on a phone against staging.

## Things the founder should know first

- **The size is wrong: this is four pull requests, not one M.** It touches a new table, a private file bucket, a public write function, a new tutor screen and a change to the parent page. Order, all under this one spec:
  - **Part A** (criteria 1 to 15): database and the `submit-claim` function, with the UPI reference as the proof. Nothing on screen changes.
  - **Part B** (criteria 16 to 29): the tutor's side: the "to confirm" chip on Home, the inbox, Confirm and Not received. Needs A. Testable with claims seeded in staging.
  - **Part C** (criteria 30 to 43): the parent's "I've paid" button and sheet. Needs A and B **live on production first** (criterion 43), or a parent can send a claim the tutor has no way to see.
  - **Part D** (criteria 44 to 51): the screenshot proof (private bucket, upload, tutor view). The PRD allows "reference number or screenshot", so the slice is complete only after D. A to C can ship first on the reference number alone (question 2).
- **A claim proves nothing; the tutor's eyes do.** A parent can type any 12 digits. We cannot check a UPI reference against a bank. That is why Confirm is a human tap and why the inbox says "Check your UPI app, then confirm." A parent who lies gets nothing unless the tutor taps Confirm.
- **Nobody tells the parent anything.** The PRD says rejecting "notifies nothing to parent". Confirming does not message the parent either: the free plan sends nothing by itself. The parent sees the receipt only when she reopens her link, or when the tutor taps "Send receipt" (R1-7). The page tells her this at the moment she sends the claim. A parent whose claim was rejected sees the fee still owing and no reason.
- **The tutor learns of a claim only when she opens the app.** "In-app notification" in the PRD means the inbox and a count on Home. There is no push, no WhatsApp message and no email to the tutor, because none is in the PRD. A tutor who does not open the app for three days sees nothing for three days. Question 3.
- **The privacy notice does not cover this.** The consent screen the parent agrees to (R1-9) says what is kept: phone number, child's name, fee and attendance. A UPI reference and above all a screenshot (it can show the parent's bank, name and phone number) are new. The founder (or a lawyer) must decide the notice wording and whether it gets a new version, which makes every parent agree again. This blocks releasing Parts C and D to production (question 4).
- **Screenshots are the riskiest data we will hold.** No retention rule is in the PRD. Default below: kept until the student or the account is deleted. Question 5 asks for a shorter limit.
- **Amount: the claim is for the total the page showed.** That matches the big Pay button (R1-9 default 2), so a parent who pays the whole total taps "I've paid" once. If that total spans several fees, Confirm records one payment and one receipt per fee, oldest first. The simpler alternative (claim only the oldest fee) would not match what the parent paid. Question 1.
- **Three canvas differences, on purpose:** Confirm is black, not green (DESIGN.md: black is for recording, green is never a button; same decision as R1-7); the button the PRD calls Reject reads "Not received" as drawn; the "SENT" in the Earlier list (`CONFIRMED 06 OCT · NO. 214 SENT`) is not shown, because the app cannot know that a receipt was sent.
- **The data model needs two columns the PRD table lacks:** when the claim was handled, and a way to tell which payments a Confirm created (so a tutor can see the receipt number in Earlier). `tech-lead` decides how. No new table: the inbox is the list of claims, not a separate notifications table.
- **R1-9 tests change on purpose.** R1-9's database tests pin the exact keys of the pay-page answer and ban the word "claim" in it. This slice adds one key (criterion 15). `backend-dev` edits those tests deliberately and says so in the pull request.

## 1. Goal

A parent who has already paid taps "I've paid" on her pay page, gives one proof (the 12-digit UPI reference, or a screenshot), and the tutor sees "Ravi's parent says ₹1,000 paid. Confirm?" in the app. One tap on Confirm records the payment and its receipt. One tap on Not received dismisses it. Before this slice a parent who paid has no way to say so, and the tutor has to notice the money in her UPI app and find the right student herself.

## 2. Users and moment

**The parent**: just paid in her UPI app, still on her phone, often at night, low-end Android, slow data, may read Hindi, Kannada or Telugu. She wants to be believed and to get her receipt. She will not make an account, and she must not be asked for her phone number again.

**The tutor**: between classes on her own phone. She sees a yellow "2 to confirm" on Home, opens her UPI app, finds the money, comes back, taps Confirm. She wants it faster than finding the student and using Mark paid.

## 3. Defaults chosen (no founder input needed)

Each can be reversed; none blocks Part A.

1. **The amount comes from the server, never from the parent's phone.** When the claim is sent, the database adds up what the student still owes at that moment. The page shows the same total, but the number the parent's phone sends is never used.
2. **A claim is attached to the oldest open fee and carries the total.** Confirm applies it oldest first, one payment and one receipt per fee it covers, by the same rules and the same receipt counter as Mark paid (R1-7). (Question 1.)
3. **Confirm records method UPI, paid on the India day the claim was sent.** If that day is more than 90 days back, Confirm is refused (R1-7's 90-day rule) and the card says to use Mark paid.
4. **Confirm never records more than is owed.** If the tutor marked the fee paid herself in the meantime, or part paid it, so that less is owed than the claim says, Confirm is refused with "Only {amount} is open now" and the card stays. She taps Not received or uses Mark paid. The tutor cannot change the amount on a claim in one tap. (Question 6.)
5. **One pending claim per student.** While one is waiting, the parent's page shows "waiting for {tutor} to confirm" and the "I've paid" button is not drawn. This also caps what a stranger with a leaked link can put in a tutor's inbox.
6. **A UPI reference can be used once per tutor.** A reference already on a pending or confirmed claim of the same tutor is refused ("This reference was already sent"). This stops one payment being claimed against next month's fee. A rejected claim does not block its reference.
7. **The reference is 12 digits.** The parent may type spaces or hyphens; they are removed and only the 12 digits are stored. Letters, fewer or more digits are refused.
8. **Claims need consent first.** The button exists only after the parent has agreed on the pay page, and the server refuses a claim for a student without a consent record for the current notice.
9. **Not received is final and silent.** It asks once ("Ravi's parent will not be told."), marks the claim rejected and tells no one. It does not block Mark paid on the same fee later. A confirmed or rejected claim is never reopened. Undoing the payment later (R1-7, 24 hours) leaves the claim "confirmed"; there is no fourth status.
10. **The inbox shows newest first, with the last 20 handled claims under "Earlier".** Older ones are not shown. A claim never expires on its own.
11. **No push, no realtime, no polling.** The count on Home is read when Home loads and when the app comes back to the foreground.
12. **Tutor screens need network** (no outbox before R1-12): offline, Confirm and Not received are refused with R1-7's message and nothing is saved. The parent's page also needs network and never queues.
13. **Words:** parent-side words are in the `payPage` namespace and ship in all four languages like the rest of the page; tutor-side words are in a new `claims` namespace. English is final; hi, kn and te are `TODO-translate` until reviewed, and the parent never sees `TODO-translate` (R1-9 criterion 36 holds). Since 2026-10-16 the claim sheet is English-only, like the rest of `/p/`, until a named person signs a language (`docs/specs/parent-pages-english.md`).
14. **Screenshots (Part D):** JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 2 MB, shrunk on the parent's phone to a longest side of 1280px before sending (this also drops location data). Shown to the tutor through a link that works for 60 seconds. Kept until the student or account is deleted (question 5).
15. **Analytics:** none fires in this slice; see section 8.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

PRD criteria, word for word:

- "'I've paid' (parent self-report): Parent taps 'I've paid' → enters UPI reference number (12 digits) or uploads a screenshot → creates `payment_claims` row (status pending) → tutor gets an in-app notification 'Ravi's parent says ₹1,000 paid. Confirm?' → one tap Confirm creates the payment + receipt; Reject notifies nothing to parent but marks claim rejected"
- "Parent pay page ... 'I've paid' button (below) ..."
- "`payment_claims(id, tutor_id, due_id, amount, upi_ref, screenshot_path, status ('pending'|'confirmed'|'rejected'), created_at)`"
- "Screenshot uploads go to a private storage bucket; only the owning tutor can read them."
- "Public pages (`/p/`, `/r/`) never query tables directly; they call Edge Functions that look up by unguessable token ... and return only what that page needs."
- "Money is stored as integer paise. All tutor-owned tables carry `tutor_id`." and "row-level security on every table"
- Analytics: `claim_submitted`, `claim_confirmed`.

Tests use fake Supabase and function hosts and a fixed clock of 2026-10-12 05:00 UTC (10:30 India) unless stated.

### Part A: database and the submit function

1. [db] `payment_claims` exists with the PRD columns: `id`, `tutor_id`, `due_id`, `amount` (integer paise, above 0), `upi_ref` (null or exactly 12 digits), `screenshot_path` (null until Part D), `status` in (`pending`, `confirmed`, `rejected`) default `pending`, `created_at`; plus when it was handled. A check says at least one of `upi_ref` and `screenshot_path` is present. Composite foreign key `(tutor_id, due_id)` to `dues`, so a claim always names a fee of the same tutor. No parent name, phone or any other personal column.
2. [db] RLS on, one select policy `tutor_id = auth.uid()`. No insert, update or delete grant to any client role; `anon` has no privilege at all. Tutor B reads zero of tutor A's claims by any filter or join. Deleting the tutor or the student removes their claims. A **pending** claim freezes the fee it names, as a payment does: changing or clearing the student's "Already owes" does not change or delete that earlier-balance fee while the claim waits (section 17). A fee is deleted only by that trigger (an earlier balance cleared, with no payment and no pending claim), and its handled claims go with it. A pgTAP file proves this with two tutors and an anonymous session, as `payments` does.
3. [db] Status moves only `pending` to `confirmed` or `pending` to `rejected`, once. A trigger refuses any other change, including editing `amount`, `upi_ref` or `due_id` after insert, as `payments` does.
4. [db] At most one `pending` claim per student (default 5), enforced by the database, not only by the function. Two submissions at the same moment make one row.
5. [db] `submit_claim(p_token, p_upi_ref, p_screenshot_path)` is executable by `service_role` only (not `anon`, not `authenticated`, not public). It looks up `students.pay_token` exactly. It returns a single code and no data: `ok`, `not_found` (unknown, malformed, archived or tutor-less: same answer), `consent_needed`, `nothing_owed` (no open fee), `bad_reference`, `no_proof`, `duplicate_reference`, `already_pending`. Every refusal writes nothing.
6. [db] On `ok`: `amount` is the sum still owed over all the student's open fees at that moment, `due_id` is the oldest open fee (an earlier balance first), status `pending`. The amount is computed in SQL; the function has no amount argument. A student with a fee of ₹10,00,000 and one of ₹1: amounts are exact paise, no float.
7. [db] The reference is normalised before it is stored (spaces and hyphens removed) and must then be 12 digits. `6281 0473 9915`, `6281-0473-9915` and `628104739915` are one reference. 11 digits, 13 digits, letters, empty with no screenshot: refused. A reference already on a pending or confirmed claim of the same tutor: `duplicate_reference`; on a rejected claim: accepted.
8. [db] `confirm_claim(p_claim, p_request_id)` is executable by signed-in users only. In one transaction it checks the claim is the caller's and pending, locks the tutor's receipt counter as `record_payment` does, then records one payment per open fee, oldest first, until the claimed amount is used up: method `upi`, `paid_on` the India day the claim was created, each with its own sequential receipt number from the same counter as Mark paid, and each due's `amount_paid` and `status` updated. Then it marks the claim `confirmed`. It reuses R1-7's rules and receipt numbering, not a second copy of them (`tech-lead` decides how).
9. [db] Confirm refuses, changes nothing and does not use a receipt number when: the claim is not the caller's or does not exist (same answer, `claim_not_found`), it is already confirmed or rejected (`already_handled`), the claimed amount is more than what is owed now (`amount_over_remaining`, with the amount owed in the error detail), the student is archived, or the claim was created more than 90 days ago (`date_out_of_range`). Anonymous callers are refused.
10. [db] Confirm is safe to repeat: the same request id twice makes one set of payments and returns the same result. A claim for three fees makes three payments whose amounts add to the claim exactly, and receipt numbers with no gap. Confirm racing with Mark paid on the same fee (two sessions): never more than the fee is recorded; the loser is refused. Two confirms of one claim at once: one set of payments.
11. [db] `reject_claim(p_claim)` is executable by signed-in users only: own pending claim to `rejected`, no payment, no change to any due or receipt number. Again, or another tutor's claim: refused with no change and no hint which.
12. [db] `020_public_tables_have_rls.sql`, `030_default_privileges.sql` and `080_grants_after_migration.sql` still pass with the new table and functions in their lists, not weakened. `pnpm check:db --base origin/main` passes. The migration stamp is later than every migration on `main`.
13. [fn] Edge Function `submit-claim`, in the `pay-page` pattern: POST only, token and `upi_ref` in the JSON body (never the URL), 405 otherwise, CORS from the allowed hosts only, body over 512 bytes gives 413, bad JSON gives 400, body keys exactly `{token, upi_ref}` (Part D adds one). It reads no amount, student id or time from the caller. Malformed, short and unknown tokens give the same 404 body, byte for byte, and a malformed one still performs one lookup with a dummy token. The refusal codes map to fixed statuses and a `{code}` body (`ok` 200, `bad_reference` and `no_proof` 422, `duplicate_reference`, `already_pending` and `nothing_owed` 409, `consent_needed` 403). The input check is the one shared pure file the page also imports, with one vectors file (supabase/CLAUDE.md rule).
14. [fn] Limits: 30 requests per hashed IP per hour (a larger shared bucket when the platform gives no IP, as `receipt`), 10 per hashed token per hour; the next gets 429 with `Retry-After`. The raw IP and raw token never reach counters, logs or storage (spy test). Salt is a new secret `CLAIM_HASH_SALT`, different from every other salt. `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, `verify_jwt = false`. Logs are fixed words (`ok`, `not_found`, `rate_limited`, `refused`, `error`), never a token, reference or amount. A thrown dependency gives 500 with no detail.
15. [db] `get_pay_page` adds exactly one key after consent and none before: `pending_claim`, null or an object with exactly `amount` and `submitted_on` (India day). It never returns the reference, screenshot, claim id or status. The R1-9 key-list test is updated to the new literal and its "no word claim" check is narrowed to allow only this key, in a visible change. Pre-consent answer unchanged.

### Part B: the tutor's inbox

16. [e2e] **Entry.** Home shows a yellow "{n} to confirm" chip (canvas `Home`, token colours, 44px high, with the offset shadow) when at least one claim is pending, and nothing when none is (R1-5 and R1-6 left it out until now). It opens the inbox. The count is read when Home loads and when the app returns to the foreground. It never polls. It never shows a number the database has not returned.
17. [e2e] **Inbox** (`Claims`, PRD screen 12), reached only from that chip and from its own address. Back arrow (44px) to Home. Heading "{n} parents say they've paid" (one: "1 parent says they've paid"), line "Check your UPI app, then confirm.".
18. [e2e] Each pending claim is a card: when it was sent (India time, "Today 4:12 pm", "Yesterday 9:40 pm", else `DD MMM YYYY`) with the fee month (or "Earlier balance", or "{n} fees" when the claim spans more than one); the student's name; the amount in the headline face (`₹1,200`, 0-9 digits, fitted, never cut); then the proof row; then the buttons. The notification wording of the PRD is the card's accessible name: "{name}'s parent says {amount} paid. Confirm?".
19. [e2e] **Proof row.** A UPI reference shows in groups of four in the mono face (`6281 0473 9915`) with a "Copy" button (44px); Copy puts the 12 digits on the clipboard and says "Copied" for two seconds, and is not drawn when the browser has no clipboard. A screenshot shows "Screenshot" and a "View" button (Part D). Newest first.
20. [e2e] **Confirm paid** (black, 52px, offset shadow): "Saving…", a second tap is ignored, and the card leaves the inbox only after the database has confirmed (no optimistic result). Then the card appears under Earlier as `CONFIRMED {DD MMM} · NO. {n}` (first number and "and {k} more" when the claim made several), the count on Home and in the heading drops, and the R1-7 undo banner shows with "Undo" and "Send receipt" for 10 seconds. Undo and Send receipt behave as in R1-7. The board row for that fee moves to Paid (or stays Part paid) without a reload.
21. [e2e] **Not received** (outline, 52px, 104px wide as drawn, label never cut): asks once, "Not received? {name}'s parent will not be told." with Cancel and a confirm button; on confirm the claim leaves the inbox and appears under Earlier as `NOT RECEIVED {DD MMM}`. No payment is recorded, the fee is unchanged, nothing is sent to the parent.
22. [e2e] **Refusals** show a message on the card itself (never a toast alone) and leave the card: "Only {amount} is open now. Mark it paid yourself or tap Not received." (the claim is now larger than what is owed); "Already handled." (another phone did it; the list refreshes); "{name} is archived. Restore the student to confirm." (Confirm disabled, Not received still works); "This was sent over 90 days ago. Use Mark paid on the fee." (Confirm disabled).
23. [e2e] **Failure and offline.** A failed request: "Couldn't save. Nothing was recorded. Try again." and the same request id is reused on Try again, so one tap that saved but lost its answer never records twice. Offline: tapping Confirm or Not received shows R1-7's "You are offline. Payments need a connection for now. Nothing was saved." and sends nothing.
24. [e2e] **Two phones.** Phone 2 confirms; phone 1, stale, taps Confirm on the same card: it sees "Already handled." and no second payment exists. Phone 1 taps Mark paid on the fee while phone 2 holds the claim: the later of the two is refused, never double-recorded.
25. [e2e] **Earlier** lists the last 20 handled claims: name, `CONFIRMED {date} · NO. {n}` or `NOT RECEIVED {date}`, amount. Rows are 56px or more. A confirmed claim whose payment was later undone still says confirmed.
26. [e2e] **Empty:** with nothing pending the inbox says "Nothing to confirm right now." and still lists Earlier. The Home chip is gone. A student with an 80-character name and a claim for ₹10,00,000 wrap and fit.
27. [e2e] The tutor's app reads claims only through RLS (select of named columns, never `*`, never `tutor_id`) and writes only through `confirm_claim` and `reject_claim`, from one small file, as `payments.ts` does. No request writes `payment_claims` or `payments` directly. Only the app's host and the Supabase host are contacted.
28. [e2e] Layout at 360px and the largest system font, in en, hi, kn and te: no sideways scroll, every control at least 44px, no text under 14px, nothing cut with an ellipsis, Confirm reachable on a card with the longest name and the longest tutor-side words. Status is a word plus a mark in grayscale (`CONFIRMED`, `NOT RECEIVED` each carry a mark). The route's first JS is at most 200 KB gzipped; `home` stays within R1-7's 185 KB ceiling (`pnpm size`; measure the chip's cost and report it).
29. [e2e] On a wide window the chip and the inbox use the same single column pattern as the rest of the app. The "To confirm" nav item and "Parents say paid" tile drawn in `Home-desktop` belong to the two-pane desktop board (D1b) and are added by it, not here (question 7).

### Part D: the screenshot

44. [fn] `submit-claim` accepts one screenshot with the claim: JPEG, PNG or WebP, decided from the file's first bytes and not its name or declared type; at most 2 MB (413 over, 415 wrong type, same fixed bodies); one file only. A reference alone, a screenshot alone or both are accepted; neither is `no_proof`. The body limit and limits of criterion 14 stay, with a larger size limit for this one shape only.
45. [db] Screenshots live in a **private** storage bucket, path starting with the tutor's id, no public URL. Storage policies: the owning tutor can select her own objects; no client role can insert, update or delete; `anon` can do nothing; tutor B cannot read tutor A's file (pgTAP or storage test, two tutors and anonymous). The function writes with the service role only after the claim row exists or in one step with it; a refused or failed claim leaves no file behind (test).
46. [db] `screenshot_path` is set by the function only; deleting the claim, the student or the tutor's account removes the file.
47. [e2e] **Parent side.** "Attach payment screenshot" (min 64px, dashed, `Only {tutor} can see it` under it, as drawn) opens the phone's image picker (images only). A chosen file shows as a small preview (64px) with its name cut to fit and a "Remove" button (44px). The picture is shrunk on the phone (longest side 1280px) before it is sent, so a 6 MB camera photo works; a file that is not an image, or still over 2 MB after shrinking, is refused with a message beside the button. Reference, screenshot or both satisfy "one proof".
48. [e2e] Sending shows "Uploading…" and a second tap is ignored. A slow or failed upload keeps the preview and the typed reference and says "Couldn't send. Nothing was saved. Try again." After 30 seconds the parent is told it is still trying and can cancel.
49. [e2e] **Tutor side.** "View" opens the screenshot from a short-lived link (60 seconds) made when she taps, in the app, with a "Close" button (44px); the link is not stored, not logged and not shared. An expired link or a missing file says "Couldn't open the screenshot." and the card still has Confirm and Not received. The picture is never in the board, the list or any cache the service worker keeps (R1-12 note).
50. [unit] Resizing happens in the browser with the canvas the browser already has, no library; if the browser cannot do it, the original is sent when it is under 2 MB and refused otherwise. EXIF and other metadata are not sent when the resize ran.
51. [manual] Part D is released only after the retention decision (question 5) is implemented or explicitly deferred by the founder in writing in the pull request.

## 5. Edge cases

- **Empty:** tutor with no claims: no chip, inbox says nothing to confirm. Parent with nothing owed: no button.
- **Offline or slow network:** criteria 23, 36, 48. Nothing is queued on either side; nothing is shown as saved until the database says so.
- **Very long names, large font, second language:** criteria 26, 28, 40. Tutor in Telugu, parent in Hindi: the inbox is Telugu, the claim sheet is Hindi, the amounts and the reference stay 0-9.
- **Partial payment:** the claim is for what the page showed. A part-paid fee claims its remaining amount. A parent who paid less than the page said cannot change the amount; the tutor taps Not received and uses Mark paid for the real amount (default 4, question 6).
- **Parent pays, claims, and the tutor also marks it paid herself:** whichever happens second is refused; the card says "Only {amount} is open now" or the fee is paid and Confirm is refused. No double payment is recorded. The tutor taps Not received to clear the card.
- **Duplicates:** two taps on Send make one claim. Sending again while one waits: nothing new, the page shows the pending note. Same reference on two students of one tutor: refused the second time. A parent who pays twice by mistake is not handled here (R1-7 refuses over-payment).
- **Parent opening an old link:** the link shows what is owed now. A claim sent from an old tab for a fee that is since paid gets "nothing is owed", the page refreshes to ALL PAID.
- **Claim for a fee, then the monthly fee grows:** the claim keeps the amount it was sent with; Confirm applies it and any extra owed stays open.
- **Notice version changes while the sheet is open:** the server refuses with `consent_needed`; the page reloads to the consent screen. What she typed is lost; accepted.
- **Student archived, tutor deleted:** a new claim gets the "link doesn't work" answer. Existing claims of an archived student stay in the inbox with Confirm disabled; deleting the account removes claims and files.
- **Tutor clears or changes "Already owes" while a claim on the earlier balance waits:** the earlier-balance fee and the claim stay exactly as they were (the database refuses to change or delete a fee a pending claim names, as it already does for a fee with a payment). The edit screen shows "Already owes" read-only with a line pointing to the claim, so she is never silently overruled; once she confirms or taps Not received, the field unlocks (unless Confirm recorded a payment, which locks it for good as in R1-7). Section 17.
- **Guessing tokens, flooding:** criteria 13, 14 and default 5 (one pending claim per student) bound what a stranger can do.
- **Tutor ignores a claim for months:** it stays pending, the chip keeps counting it, Confirm becomes unavailable after 90 days (default 3).
- **Parent sends a wrong or made-up reference:** it is accepted if it has 12 digits. The tutor cannot be helped by the app here; she checks her UPI app and taps Not received.
- **Tutor with no UPI ID:** claims still work (the parent may have paid by the plain ID in the reminder, or in cash with a reference of her own).
- **Undo after Confirm:** the claim stays confirmed; the tutor can Mark paid again by hand.
- **In-app browsers (WhatsApp, Instagram):** the sheet and keypad must work inside them; the file picker may not (Part D, manual step 15).
- **A claim for several fees and Send receipt:** the banner's Send receipt opens the receipt of the last payment made; the others are reachable from each fee's Receipt button (R1-7). If this is clumsy in the first week with real tutors, question 1's alternative is the fix.
- **App service worker (R1-12):** it must never cache the `submit-claim` answers, signed screenshot links or the inbox of another tutor on a shared phone. Noted for R1-12.

### 1. Approach

One table, `payment_claims`, written only by three database functions: `submit_claim` (service role only, called by the new `submit-claim` Edge Function), and `confirm_claim` and `reject_claim` (signed-in tutor). `confirm_claim` does not copy any money rule: inside one transaction it takes the tutor's receipt-counter lock first (the same lock `record_payment` takes), then calls `record_payment` itself once per fee, oldest first, so every check, the 90-day rule, the numbering and the due update stay in the one place they already live. The tutor's inbox reads claims through RLS and writes only through the two functions; the parent's page gets one extra key from `get_pay_page`. The screenshot (Part D) travels to the function in the same request, is checked by its first bytes, and is stored in a private bucket under the tutor's id before the claim row is written; a refused claim deletes it.

### 2. Decisions the spec left to me

1. **Linking a claim to the payments it created: two columns on the claim, not a link table and not a column on `payments`.** `confirm_claim` holds the counter lock from its first `record_payment` call to the end of the transaction, so the receipt numbers it takes are consecutive. The claim stores `confirmed_receipt_from` and `confirmed_receipt_count`; the app finds the payments with `receipt_no >= from and < from + count` (one read of `payments`, which it can already do). Rejected: `payments.claim_id` (changes `record_payment`'s insert and the `payments_immutable` trigger, the money code, for a display need) and a link table (a second new table to test). Cost: the link is by number range, so a test must prove the range is gap-free (criterion 10).
2. **Each payment's request id is derived, not stored.** `confirm_claim(p_claim, p_request_id)` passes `md5(p_request_id::text || ':' || n)::uuid` (n = 1, 2, ...) to `record_payment`. A retry never reaches that code: the claim is already `confirmed` with the same `confirm_request_id`, so `confirm_claim` returns the stored claim row unchanged (the same idea as `record_payment` step 4). A retry with a different request id on a confirmed or rejected claim gets `already_handled`.
3. **Two columns the PRD table lacks, plus two for the receipts, plus `fee_count`.** `student_id` (needed for "one pending claim per student" and for the Home name, no personal data), `handled_at`, `confirm_request_id`, `confirmed_receipt_from`, `confirmed_receipt_count`, and `fee_count` (how many open fees the amount spanned when it was sent, for the card's "{n} fees"). `amount` comes from the same open-dues query that decides `due_id`, as `bigint` in SQL, then capped at 2,000,000,000 paise to fit the `integer` column (a total that large is already the page's "too big" case; Confirm applies oldest first until the claim is used up, so a capped claim is still applied correctly).
4. **Foreign keys are stricter than the spec asks, on purpose.** `(tutor_id, student_id)` references `students (tutor_id, id)` `on delete cascade`, and `(tutor_id, student_id, due_id)` references `dues (tutor_id, student_id, id)` `on delete cascade`. The second needs one additive key on `dues`: `unique (tutor_id, student_id, id)` (same pattern as R1-7's `dues_tutor_id_id_key`). It makes it impossible for a claim to name a fee of another student, not only of another tutor. The spec's `(tutor_id, due_id)` rule is met by it.
5. **One definition of "open fees, oldest first".** An internal function `public.open_dues_oldest_first(p_tutor uuid, p_student uuid)` returns the open dues in the order `get_pay_page` already uses (`kind <> 'opening'`, `due_date`, `month`). `submit_claim` and `confirm_claim` both call it. It is revoked from every client role. A pgTAP test pins that its order equals `get_pay_page`'s `open_dues` order for a student with an earlier balance and three months.
6. **Screenshot path (D) is made by the function, not by SQL.** `submit_claim`'s third argument stays the path (as the spec names it). A service-only helper `claim_upload_target(p_token)` returns the tutor id for a valid, active token (or null) so the function can build `<tutor_id>/<random uuid>.<ext>` and upload before the claim exists. The function deletes the object if `submit_claim` answers anything but `ok`.
7. **This plan overrides one line of `supabase/CLAUDE.md`.** That file says the pay page "gets a short-lived signed upload URL from the Edge Function". The spec (criteria 39 and 44) has the file sent to the function itself, which lets the function check the file's first bytes (a signed upload URL cannot) and keeps the parent page talking to the functions host only. Part D rewrites that line. `lead-reviewer`: this is deliberate.
8. **Undo after a multi-fee Confirm.** The R1-7 banner voids one payment. For a claim of several payments, Undo voids them newest first with `void_payment`, one call each, and stops at the first failure with the R1-7 failure message, naming that some may remain recorded (the fee's Receipt button undoes each one). Single-fee claims behave exactly as R1-7. No new database function. Send receipt uses the last payment made, as the spec says (section 5, last bullet).

### 11. Risks and traps

1. **Money: Confirm must never use a number it did not record, and never record more than owed.** Plan: the counter lock first, then `record_payment` for every payment (so its checks decide), one transaction, so any failure rolls back payments and counter. The trap is writing a second copy of the rules in `confirm_claim`; the plan has none, and the 280 file would fail if the two diverged. A second trap: calling `record_payment` with a request id that collides with a Mark paid retry; the derived id is a hash of the claim's own random request id and the position.
2. **Lock order and deadlock.** `record_payment` takes counter then due. `confirm_claim` takes counter, then the claim, then (inside `record_payment`) the dues. `submit_claim` locks only the student row and never the counter. `reject_claim` locks only the claim. No function takes a lock in the opposite order. The concurrency script proves two confirms and confirm-versus-Mark-paid.
3. **A public write endpoint.** What a stranger with a leaked link can do is bounded by: one pending claim per student (DB index), per-IP 30 and per-token 10 an hour, 512-byte bodies, consent required, a reference that is 12 ASCII digits and unique per tutor. A scraper guessing tokens gets the same 404 body for every miss and one dummy lookup for a malformed token. The hash for IP and token uses a new salt `CLAIM_HASH_SALT`; do not reuse the pay-page one.
4. **Information leak on `get_pay_page`.** Only `amount` and `submitted_on`; the pgTAP key-list test is the guard. A `stable` function must stay read only.
5. **Unicode digits.** `\d` in Postgres and in some engines matches non-ASCII digits. The checks use `[0-9]` and the vectors include Devanagari, Kannada, Telugu and full-width digits that must be refused by both sides.
6. **Integer overflow.** A sum over many fees can pass 2,147,483,647 paise; the function sums in `bigint` and caps the column value, as `get_pay_page` reads `total_remaining` as `bigint`. Test with 22 fees at the cap.
7. **Home count showing a stale or invented number.** Read on mount and when the page is visible again; render nothing while loading or on failure; the e2e test that returns a different count after a background and foreground proves it.
8. **Claim order versus page order.** The page's Pay total and the claim must agree. One shared `open_dues_oldest_first` and a test against `get_pay_page` prevents a silent drift.
9. **Multi-fee Undo** (decision 8): partial failure leaves some payments recorded. The message says so and the fee's own Receipt button can undo each. Flagged to `test-engineer` and `code-reviewer`.
10. **Storage deletion is not a SQL delete.** `delete from storage.objects` removes metadata only. The plan queues paths by trigger and deletes through the Storage API in a function; without the sweep (D-Q1 "no") the files outlive deleted students until R1-13. This is the biggest open item in D.
11. **Screenshots in caches.** The signed URL is created on tap, held in state, never stored. The R1-12 service worker must not cache `/storage/v1/` or `submit-claim`. Noted in the PR body for R1-12.
12. **Edge Function memory and time on a 2 MB upload.** The body is read with a hard cap and a 2 MB image fits easily; the cap, not trust in `Content-Length`, is the guard (declared and streamed size both checked, as `readLimited`).
13. **Migration drift and order.** Stamp later than `20261012230218` and any newer migration on `main`; the D migration after A's. A migration must work with the front end and functions live now: A's `get_pay_page` change is invisible to the live function (allow-list), and D replaces `submit_claim` with the same signature so the A function keeps working until the D function deploys.
14. **R1-9 tests changed on purpose** (section 3). The risk is loosening them to pass. Rule for `backend-dev`: narrow the "claim" word ban to exactly `pending_claim`, do not delete it, and say so in the PR.
15. **Releasing C before B.** A parent could send a claim the tutor cannot see. Part C is not merged until A and B are on production and the founder has seen a staging-to-inbox claim. Also criterion 43 and question 4 (the privacy notice) block C on production, even though the code can merge earlier on a branch.
16. **Page budget.** The `p` shell has about 8 KB of room under 151 KB; four languages of new words may use most of it. Measure at task 10 before writing the e2e.
